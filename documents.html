Plain-English disclosures

How on-demand documents work, legally.

Asbury Law is a Florida law firm. Each on-demand document is provided as limited-scope legal services under Rule 4-1.2(c) of the Rules Regulating The Florida Bar, under a written engagement you accept before the document is generated. You choose which document to use and enter the information yourself; before purchase you can review a blank sample of each template, and before download you can review the completed draft. Basic-tier documents are not individually reviewed unless you select Premium or Attorney-Reviewed.

Every template is designed for Florida law and reviewed by a Florida-licensed attorney. It is not warranted for use in any other state. Purchasing a document creates a limited attorney-client relationship for that document only; it does not include ongoing representation, court appearances, negotiation, or filing. No particular legal or financial result is promised. Fees are flat, stated before purchase, and earned on delivery; refund terms are stated in the Terms of Service and the engagement agreement.
