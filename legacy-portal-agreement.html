This Subscription & Vault Agreement (the "Agreement") is between you and Legacy Portal, LLC ("Legacy Portal," "we," "us"), operator of the Legacy Vault (the "Service"). By clicking "I agree" or subscribing, you accept this Agreement.

1. The Service

Legacy Portal provides an individual online vault where each subscriber may upload, organize, store, and retrieve digital copies of documents, images, videos, and other files — personal, family, or business — with designee and alert features. The Service is storage and organization only. We do not review your files for legal sufficiency, tell you which documents you need, or prepare documents.

2. Eligibility and account

You must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract. You are responsible for the accuracy of your account information, the security of your credentials, and all activity in your vault. Notices are sent to the email address on your account; keep it current.

3. Plans, fees, and introductory pricing

Current plans and prices are published at checkout. Clients of Asbury Law receive the vault included for the first three months of an estate plan engagement and included for the duration of any Attorney on Retainer membership; otherwise, after any introductory period your plan renews at its regular published rate. A valid payment method is required at signup. We may change prices prospectively with at least 30 days' notice to your account email. Verified military and veteran discounts may be available as published.

4. Automatic billing authorization

Recurring-charge authorization. By subscribing, you authorize Legacy Portal and its payment processor to store your payment method and automatically charge it the subscription fee, plus applicable taxes and any fees due under this Agreement, at the start of each billing cycle, continuing until you cancel. For annual plans we send a renewal reminder at least 15 days before the renewal charge. You may update your payment method or cancel online at any time; cancellation stops future charges but does not refund the current cycle except as stated in Section 9.

7. Your content and our license

Your files are yours. You grant us only the limited license necessary to host, encrypt, back up, display to you, and deliver your files as you direct. We do not sell your content, mine it, or use it for advertising or AI training.

8. Retention and deletion after suspension

For 12 months after suspension your files remain stored and retrievable under Section 6 and your account remains reinstatable. After 12 months we send a final deletion notice; if you do not reinstate or complete a retrieval within 30 days, your files become eligible for permanent deletion. Minimal account and billing records may be retained as required by law.

9. Cancellation and free export window

Cancel online at any time; cancellation is effective at the end of the current paid cycle. For 30 days after a voluntary cancellation in good standing you may export all of your files free of charge. Fees already paid are non-refundable except where required by law or stated here. If we discontinue the Service we give at least 60 days' notice and a free export window.

10. Designees, alerts, and conditional access

You may designate backup administrators, trusted contacts, and beneficiaries and set each person's role. Alerts notify designees that a file exists or has been updated — never its contents. Designee access occurs only through the mechanism you configure: your in-app authorization, or presentation of verification you specify in advance (for example, a certified death certificate together with identity verification). We may decline access where verification is incomplete or fraud is suspected.

11. Security

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest with zero-access encryption on a Swiss-domiciled provider governed by Swiss data-protection law, with administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to a private storage service. No system is perfectly secure; protect your credentials and enable available account-security features. The vault is a technical and contractual security measure; it does not shield materials from a valid legal process.

12. Acceptable use

You may not store unlawful content, malware, or content you have no right to possess, or probe or disrupt the Service.
