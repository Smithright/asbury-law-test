Questions

What people ask before they start.What people ask before they start.

Do I really never have to come to an office?

Correct. Intake, review, questions, signing, witnessing, and notarization all happen online. Florida law has permitted remote online notarization and remote witnessing of estate planning documents since 2020, and Asbury Law uses an integrated, Florida-authorized online execution ceremony for every signing. If you'd prefer to sign in person, the Jacksonville office is available by appointment.

What does "Florida-authorized online ceremony" mean?

Florida's remote online notarization law (Chapter 117, Part II, Florida Statutes) allows a Florida online notary, using a platform registered with the Florida Department of State, to notarize your documents over a recorded audio-video session after verifying your identity. Florida also allows witnesses to attend remotely under supervision. The result is a fully valid Florida execution — the same legal effect as signing at a conference table, without the conference table.

What's the difference between a will and a living trust?

A will goes through probate — a court process that is public, takes months, and costs money. A living trust passes assets to your beneficiaries privately, without a court, typically within weeks. If you own a home in Florida, Cornerstone (which includes a trust and the deed to fund it) is usually the better fit.

What does "complimentary revision" mean?

Families change — marriages, births, moves, a business sold. For twenty years Thomas Asbury has told clients to revisit their plan every three to five years. So each plan includes one full revision at no charge within its window: 3 years for Foundations, 4 for Cornerstone, 5 for Pinnacle. Living Plan Updates extends that to every future revision, and members get updates included.

Are these plans really drafted by an attorney, not software?

Yes. Software collects your answers; Thomas Asbury reads them, asks follow-up questions, and drafts and signs off on every document. The on-demand documents opening in October are different — those are self-service at the Basic tier, which is why they're clearly labeled.

Can I upgrade later?

Always. Move from Foundations to Cornerstone, or Cornerstone to Pinnacle, at any time and your original flat fee is credited in full against the new plan.

How does veteran pricing work?

Upload a DD-214, VA card, or military ID at checkout. The 10% discount applies automatically to every plan, membership, document, and content purchase. Advance directives for veterans are free regardless of whether you purchase anything else.

Your executed documents are delivered the same day to your private, encrypted Legacy Portal, included with every plan for your first three months. After that the portal continues as a modest monthly subscription (cancel anytime), or is included with any Attorney on Retainer membership. Your documents remain yours and can be downloaded at any time; keep your signed originals.
