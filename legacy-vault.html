Private by Design

Confidentiality is the architecture,not an afterthought.

This firm was restructured from the ground up with client confidentiality, privacy, and encryption at the center. Here is how, in plain English.

Encrypted correspondence

Privileged and sensitive client communications run through end-to-end encrypted Proton Mail, governed by Swiss data-protection law. Messages are encrypted so that only you and the firm can read them — the provider cannot.

Zero-access client files

Client and matter files are held in zero-access encrypted storage (Proton Drive): files are encrypted on your device before they reach the server, and even file names are hidden. Handled by the attorney — never routed through third-party automation.

Encrypted video, by default

Substantive client sessions run on end-to-end encrypted video. Firm operations run on a single platform with encryption in transit and at rest, and confidential workflows never leave it.

No scanning, no ads, no AI training

Your materials are never mined, sold, or fed to a model. Any AI tool the firm uses runs under commercial terms that prohibit training on client data, and the attorney verifies every AI-assisted work product.

The Legacy Vault · For anyone, not just clients

One place where the people you loveknow everything is.

When a loved one dies or is incapacitated, the first crisis is usually not legal — it's that nobody knows where anything is. The Legacy Vault is a single, unified, encrypted hub for a person's most important documents, files, images, and videos — personal, family, and business — accessible 24/7 by you and by the people you designate.

  • Estate plan and advance directives, always the current valid version
  • Birth and marriage certificates, deeds, titles, insurance policies, leases
  • Contracts, operating agreements, bylaws, and confidential business files
  • A designated backup person who can access it if you cannot

The Legacy Vault is operated by Legacy Portal, LLC, a separate company affiliated with Asbury Law through common ownership. It is not a law firm and provides no legal services; storing files with it creates no attorney-client relationship or privilege and is never required to engage Asbury Law. Keep your signed originals.
