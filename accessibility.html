Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: September 25, 2026 · Asbury Law

Asbury Law is committed to making asbury-law.com usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. Our goal is to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, and we treat accessibility as part of how the site is built rather than an add-on.

What we do

  • Semantic headings, landmarks, and labeled forms so screen readers can navigate every page;
  • Keyboard access to every link, button, menu, and form control, with a visible focus indicator;
  • Text contrast that meets or exceeds WCAG AA on both the navy and cream sections of the site, and text that can be resized to 200% without loss of content;
  • Descriptive alternative text on images and captions on photographs;
  • Motion that respects your device's "reduce motion" setting;
  • No information conveyed by color alone — every status is also stated in words.

Accommodations for legal services

If you need an accommodation to review documents, complete an intake, attend a video session, or participate in an online signing ceremony — large print, additional time, a phone call instead of a form, or another format — tell us and we will arrange it at no charge. Florida's remote online notarization process includes identity verification steps; if any of them present a barrier, we will work with you and the notary provider on an alternative.

Tell us if something isn't working

Accessibility is an ongoing effort and some content may not yet meet every standard. If you encounter a barrier on this site, please send us a message with the page address and a description of the problem. We aim to respond within five business days and to fix the issue promptly.
